How it works

Pair once, like a Bluetooth keyboard.

AirPhone
Bluetooth · keyboard + mouse
Mi TV Stick
01

Find the TV's address

On the TV: Settings → Device Preferences → About → Status → Bluetooth address. (Android TV 12+ can also be found with Search nearby while its Add accessory screen is open.)

02

Pair from the phone

Open AirPhone, allow Bluetooth, type the address in Devices and tap Pair. Confirm the same code on the phone and, with the TV remote, on the TV.

03

Point and click

It connects by itself. From then on, opening the app reconnects to the last TV.

Features

Everything the TV remote does — plus a cursor.

Air mouse

Turn and tilt the phone to move the cursor — works whether you hold it flat or upright. Speed slider included.

Touchpad

Drag to move, tap to click, scroll with two fingers (or tap, then drag) — for when you'd rather not wave the phone around.

Full remote

D-pad and OK, Back, Home, Menu, Power, volume, mute and play/pause. Hold a key to repeat.

Type with your phone keyboard

Search fields fill in as you type on your phone. Turkish letters go out as their closest English letter (ş → s).

No TV app

The TV sees a standard Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. Nothing to sideload on the stick.

Private by design

No internet permission, no account, no analytics. Just Bluetooth between your phone and your TV.

Good to know

Honest limits

  • Stays connected with the screen off. While a TV is connected you'll see a "Connected to …" notification; that's what keeps the link (and the app you're using on the TV) alive. Swipe AirPhone away or tap Disconnect to end it.
  • Some phones can't do it. A few manufacturers remove Android's Bluetooth keyboard/mouse mode. AirPhone tells you on launch if yours is one of them.
  • Power puts the stick to sleep; waking it from the phone isn't guaranteed — use the TV remote or HDMI-CEC for that.
  • The TV won't list your phone. Android TV's Add accessory screen only shows keyboards, mice, remotes and speakers, so pairing always starts in AirPhone. TV reset or phone removed there? Tap ✕ on the TV in AirPhone and add it again.
